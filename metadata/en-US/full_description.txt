ALEA Softphone is the softphone for ALEA PBX, offering secure audio/video calls and messaging. With ALEA Softphone, you can be reachable at any time, even if the app is closed, with a WiFi or 3G/4G/5G internet connection.

ALEA Softphone key features:
* High definition audio and video calls
* Instant messaging with file transfer
* Secure calls and messages (encryption)
* ALEA Softphone accounts reachable at any time, even if the app is closed (thanks to push notifications)
* Call transfer, call park, conference
* Address book / directory integration

ALEA Softphone is available on the main mobile operating systems and offers advanced VoIP features (codecs, transport, encryption, DTMF…). For more information: www.aleaconnect.it

ALEA Softphone is part of the ALEA PBX product line. Contact ALEA Connect — www.aleaconnect.it — for commercial support.
